Informed Consent

In this study, you will be asked to take over driving a simulated robot on a remote autonomous mission while you are in the middle of performing a different whack-a-mole task.

First, there will be brief tutorials on the “whack-a-mole” task and the robot driving task. Next, in three sets of ten trials each, we will ask you to perform the whack-a-mole task and then prompt you to switch your attention to the robot. In one set of trials, you will have access to the simulated robot controls, and you will begin giving robot inputs as soon as you feel ready. In the other two sets of trials, you will not have access to the robot controls; instead, you will view the robot operating and use different interfaces to report when you feel ready to take over. The sets will be presented in a random order. You will also be asked to answer a few questions about yourself and your perception of the tasks.

This study is part of a MIT scientific research project. There are no anticipated risks or benefits to you aside from those normally associated with performing a task online. Your interaction with the experiment interface, including robot-driving performance and button clicks within the experiment window, will be recorded. The results of the research may be presented at scientific meetings or published in scientific journals. Your decision to complete this study is voluntary. Choosing to proceed indicates that you are at least 18 years of age and agree to participate in this study voluntarily.

If you choose not to participate, you will leave the study. If you are doing this study through Prolific, please return the assignment.
