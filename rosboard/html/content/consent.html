Informed Consent

In this study, you will be asked to take over driving a simulated robot on a remote autonomous mission while you are in the middle of performing a different whack-a-mole task.

First, there will be brief tutorials on the “whack-a-mole” task and the robot driving task. Next, in two sets of ten trials each, we will ask you to perform the whack-a-mole task and then prompt you to switch your attention to the robot. In one set of trials, you will watch the robot operating and simply report when you feel ready to take over. In the other set of trials, you will have access to the simulated robot controls, and you will begin giving robot inputs as soon as you feel ready. The sets will be presented in a random order. You will also be asked to answer a few questions about yourself and your perception of the tasks.

This study is part of a MIT scientific research project. There are no anticipated risks or benefits to you aside from those normally associated with performing a task online. Your decision to complete this study is voluntary. The results of the research may be presented at scientific meetings or published in scientific journals. Choosing to proceed indicates that you are at least 18 years of age and agree to participate in this study voluntarily.

If you choose not to participate, you will leave the study. During local testing, this will show an exit page. During the Prolific study, this will return you to Prolific or ask you to return the assignment.
