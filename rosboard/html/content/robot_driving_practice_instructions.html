How to Drive the Robot

The robot is driven by a single onscreen joystick. Click and drag on the blue joystick center to operate it. Moving the joystick forward and backward controls the robot’s forward and backward speed, while moving the joystick left and right steers the robot to the left and right.

On the next screen, you will be given an open space where you can practice driving the robot around. This practice does not count toward your study results.
