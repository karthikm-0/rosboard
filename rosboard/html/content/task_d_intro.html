Your Task With the Robot

Now that you have practiced using the joystick, your task with the robot is to take over driving the robot as soon as you feel confident that you understand the situation and can smoothly guide the robot toward its goal.

You get bonuses for taking over early, before the red disk, but they are counteracted by penalties for jerky driving or going off-route. If you are ready before the robot reaches the red disk, please take over early to save the robot's processing power. If you need more time for processing yourself, the robot will sit safely at the red disk until you are ready to give it the correct inputs to drive to the cyan disk.

When you press "Next", you will be dropped into a practice robot driving scenario. Please take over the robot as soon as you know what inputs to give it to continue its path through the red disk to the cyan disk.

Your performance in this practice does not matter, but it will give you experience taking over from a robot that is already driving.
