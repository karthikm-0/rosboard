Whack-a-Mole Instructions

When you are not being asked to control the robot, you will be presented with a “whack-a-mole” screen. When a mole (animal) appears on the screen, click it before it disappears. Your whack-a-mole score is the cumulative number of moles you click over the course of the study.

On the next screen, you can practice the “whack-a-mole” task. Your score on the practice does not matter, so you can proceed when you feel comfortable with the task. Click “Start Practice” to begin the practice, and “End Practice” to move on to the robot driving task.
