Your Task With the Robot

In the upcoming set of trials, your task with the robot is to analyze the situation like a robot driver and set a future driver up for success. Move the video playhead to the earliest point where you feel confident that you would have understood the situation and could have smoothly guided the robot toward its goal.

When actually driving the robot, the driver would have gotten bonuses for taking over early, before the red disk, but those bonuses would be counteracted by penalties for jerky driving or going off-route. If the driver needs more time for processing themselves, the robot will sit safely at the red disk until the driver is ready to give it the correct inputs to drive to the cyan disk.

To summarize, please move the video playhead to the earliest point that you think you could have given the robot inputs to smoothly continue its path through the red disk to the cyan disk.
