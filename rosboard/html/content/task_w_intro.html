Your Task With the Robot

In the upcoming set of trials, your task with the robot is to pause the robot feed and draw the path you would command for the robot to reach its goals. Pause the scenario at the earliest point where you feel confident that you have understood the situation and could have smoothly guided the robot toward its goal. Draw the path you would have the robot take.

You will get bonuses for pausing the robot feed early, before the red disk, but those bonuses are counteracted by penalties for going off-route. If you are ready before the robot reaches the red disk, you should take over early to save the robot's processing power. If you need more time for processing yourself, the robot will sit safely at the red disk until you are ready to give it the correct inputs to drive to the cyan disk.

To summarize, please PAUSE at the earliest point that you think you could have given the robot inputs to smoothly continue its path through the red disk to the cyan disk and DRAW your intended path.

The next screen will show you the controls for the PAUSE and DRAW interface. Click Next to proceed.
