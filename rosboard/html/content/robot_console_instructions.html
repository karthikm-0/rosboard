The Robot Console

This screen gives information about the robot.

  • The rectangular window on the left gives a video feed from the robot’s front camera.
  • The rectangular window on the right shows a top-down map created by the robot’s range-based sensor. The robot is located in the middle of the map, and the white dots represent obstacles detected by the robot.
  • The circular diagram shows the relative direction of the first and second goals compared to the robot’s forward direction.

You can see how the obstacles you see in the camera match up with the dots shown in the LiDAR map. Please familiarize yourself with the robot console, including how the red first-goal marker and cyan second-goal marker appear in the different views.

When you are ready, click the “Next” button to receive instructions on your first task with the robot.
